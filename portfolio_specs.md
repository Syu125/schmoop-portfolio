# Portfolio Specs

## Purpose
A storytelling-driven **one-page portfolio website** built with **React**, that introduces who I am, my motivations, my projects, and my skills through the metaphor of a **growing bean seed**. The goal is to both tell a personal story and pitch myself as a designer.

---

## Tech Stack
- **Framework:** React (with Vite or Next.js if routing/SEO needed later)
- **Styling:** TailwindCSS (utility-first, responsive design)
- **Animations:** Framer Motion (scroll-based animations, transitions)
- **Icons:** Phosphor Icons (Seed, Sprout, Flower, Bean/Plant)
- **Deployment:** Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages

---

## Structure (One-Page Flow)

### Stage 1: The Bean Seeds – Who I Am
- **Content**:
  - Current position and responsibilities.
  - Key interests: user experience, solving daily user needs, health-tech, AI in healthcare, interaction design.
  - Brief personal statement introducing myself as a curious and growth-oriented designer.
- **Design**:
  - Visual: seed underground.
  - Plant icon: **Phosphor `Seed` icon**.
  - Color palette: earthy tones (browns, muted greens).
  - Typography: bold intro line (headline), simple sans-serif body text.

### Stage 2: Germination & Sprouting (+ Environment) – Passions & Motivations
- **Content**:
  - Soil = needs of others (healthcare, health-tech challenges).
  - Water = my own experiences (personal insights, stealth project).
  - Narrative about what drives my passion and design motivations.
- **Design**:
  - Visual: sprouting seed with roots and water drops.
  - Plant icon: **Phosphor `Sprout` icon**.
  - Roots connect to keywords (accessibility, empathy, patients).
  - Water connects to personal sparks.
  - Palette brightens slightly (greens emerging).

### Stage 3: Leaf Growth & Flowering – Projects
- **Content**:
  - Showcase 2–3 projects.
  - Each project structured as:
    1. Challenge/problem.
    2. Design/research approach.
    3. Outcome/impact.
    4. Reflection (HCI principles + learnings).
  - Highlight interaction design + UX thought process.
- **Design**:
  - Visual: leaves/flowers to symbolize projects.
  - Plant icon: **Phosphor `Flower` icon**.
  - Projects displayed as cards/sections within the page (expandable or scroll-based reveal).
  - Clean grid layout with organic shape accents.
  - Bright, vibrant colors to show growth.

### Stage 4: Pod Fills & Seed Pods Mature – Skills & Hobbies
- **Content**:
  - Technical skills (tools, methods, prototyping, research).
  - Soft skills (collaboration, empathy, storytelling).
  - Hobbies/free-time activities (to humanize).
  - Closing line: metaphorical tie-in (e.g., "Like seeds, ideas grow — this is just one stage of my journey.").
- **Design**:
  - Visual: pods filled with seeds as icons for skills.
  - Plant icon: **Phosphor `Bean` or `Plant` icon** (to symbolize pods).
  - Fun accents/illustrations for hobbies.
  - Muted but warm tones for maturity.

---

## Visual + Design Guidelines
- **Theme:** Organic growth journey (seed → sprout → bloom → pods).
- **Layout:** One-page scrolling site. Each stage is a distinct scroll section, flowing in order.
- **Background:** Full-page dotted grid, inspired by a bullet journal.
- **Icons:** Use **Phosphor Icons** for consistency.
  - Stage 1: `Seed`
  - Stage 2: `Sprout`
  - Stage 3: `Flower`
  - Stage 4: `Bean` or `Plant`
- **Colors:** Natural palette (earth tones, healthcare greens/blues). Brighten progressively with each stage.
- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: clean sans-serif, bold.
  - Body: neutral sans-serif or humanist serif for warmth.
- **Navigation:**
  - Sticky/fixed nav or progress indicator (styled as a growing vine) for jumping between sections.
  - Smooth scroll between stages.

---

## Deliverables
- **One-Page Portfolio Website** with:
  - Scroll-based storytelling flow through Stages 1–4.
  - Inline case study sections for projects.
  - Skills + hobbies included as final section.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for desktop + mobile.
- **Assets:**
  - Phosphor plant icons for each stage.
  - Seed/plant illustrations (minimalist, vector-based).
  - Background: dotted grid texture (light and subtle).
